# CWS Business Model

Version: 1 — 2026-10-17. Owner-approved terms from the Negocio Listo offer work (October 2026).
Scope: how Cicero Web Studio makes money. For what CWS OS is and does, see `docs/product-definition.md`.

Read this before any Sales, Marketing, offer, pricing, or proposal work.

---

## 1. One-line model

Cicero Web Studio sells **one main offer, Negocio Listo**: a bilingual, Spanish-first monthly subscription that gets a local service business found online (website + Google Business Profile + local SEO, and AI search on the top tier). Recurring revenue (MRR) is the business; one-off projects are secondary.

## 2. Who we sell to

- **First niche:** Spanish-speaking contractors and home-service businesses (roofing, remodeling, landscaping, cleaning, HVAC, plumbing) in Cicero, Berwyn, Little Village, Pilsen, and southwest Chicago.
- **Typical buyer:** owner-operated, little or no marketing staff, gets work from calls and word of mouth, no website or a weak Google profile, can't pay $1,000+ upfront.
- **Second niche to test:** restaurants. Also open: childcare providers and other local services.
- Requests from outside the niche are accepted on the same tiers. Advertising stays niche-focused.

## 3. Positioning

- Win by being **local, Spanish-first, and in person** with one clear offer, not by being the cheapest web designer.
- One person covers web, Google, photo, video, and AI search.
- Monthly reporting in plain numbers (calls, clicks, reviews) is part of the product.
- Sell the monthly plan as the **budget-friendly path**: a website plus ongoing work for a monthly fee instead of a large upfront payment.

## 4. Offer and pricing (source of truth)

All tiers: **12-month minimum**, billed monthly in advance by autopay. Each tier includes everything below it.

| Tier | Monthly | Sign-up fee (retainer) | Includes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Básico | $150 | $300, due at signing | Bilingual website (ES + EN, written separately), hosting, Google Business Profile setup and optimization, local SEO and directory listings, 30 min of edits/month |
| Crecer (recommended) | $250 | $300, due at signing | Básico + 4 Google profile posts/month (2 ES, 2 EN), review requests and replies, missed-call text-back, 30-min monthly review session with a one-page report |
| Destacado | $350 | $400, due at signing | Crecer + AI search optimization (schema, crawler access, Bing/Apple listings), one bilingual FAQ or service page/month, monthly AI visibility check |
| Contenido (add-on) | +$300 | — | Quarterly photo/video shoot turned into monthly posts |

Other rates:

| Item | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Edits beyond 30 min/month | $100/hour, quoted first |
| Extra website page | $150/page, both languages |
| One-off custom site | From $2,500, always with a monthly care plan |

Fee rules:

- The sign-up fee is **never waived** by default. It pays for the one-time build and setup hours and is non-refundable once work starts.
- **Early termination:** the client pays a fee that covers the website build: 3 months of the plan for a one-page site, 6 months for a multi-page site (or the remaining months, if fewer).
- **Website buyout** after leaving: 6 months of the plan's fee.
- Price is locked for the first 12 months, then month to month with 30 days' notice for changes.
- **Existing-client exception:** a client whose site is already paid for may get Crecer at **$200/month** with no sign-up fee and a shorter minimum. Any other exception needs Tulio's decision.

## 5. Ownership and promises

- The client owns their domain, Google Business Profile, reviews, and supplied content. CWS owns its templates and code.
- **Never promise** rankings, call volume, or appearing in ChatGPT, Gemini, or Google AI answers. Promise the work and the monthly report.
- No fake reviews, review incentives, or review gating.
- A signed service agreement comes before any work. The English version controls; a Spanish version is provided alongside.

## 6. How clients are won

Clients come from **direct asks and referrals**, not ads or passive posting.

- Lead hook: a **free 10-minute Google profile check in Spanish**.
- Channels: in-person walk-ins on the Cicero corridor, Instagram and WhatsApp DMs, Google Maps searches for weak profiles, chambers of commerce, networking events, and referral partners (accountants, insurance agents, print shops, alumni programs).
- System support: Sales runs a daily target of 5 initial prospect emails (DEC-031); Marketing publishes owner-confirmed posts (DEC-028).
- Paid ads wait until the offer closes reliably face to face (about month 6).
- Delivery is **virtual first** (video or WhatsApp). In-person shoots are arranged case by case.

## 7. Economics and targets

| Measure | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Labor per client per month | Básico 1–1.5 hrs, Crecer 2.5–3 hrs, Destacado 4–5 hrs |
| Effective hourly rate | About $70/hr; never below the $50/hr floor |
| Next milestone | **10 subscription clients** |
| Mix | At least 50% of signups on Crecer or Destacado |
| Churn assumption | Lose about a third of clients per year, so outreach never fully stops |

Scaling notes:

- Revenue scales with client count; **owner time is the constraint**. Solo and part-time, capacity is about 15–20 clients.
- Levers, in order: cut hours per client (templates, reporting automation), raise average revenue per client (Crecer default, Destacado and Contenido upsells), raise prices for new clients, then hire part-time help around 15 clients.
- Track hours per client from the first client. Decide pricing and hiring from that data at 10 clients.

## 8. When prices change

Raise prices when any of these is true:

1. More than about half of qualified pitches close with no price pushback over about 10 pitches.
2. Capacity is near full (about 15 clients or weekly hours maxed).
3. A tier's tracked effective rate falls below target.
4. 3–5 clients have results with real numbers to show.

How: new clients first, in $50 steps (e.g. $200/$300/$400). The first 10 clients keep launch pricing for their 12 months as founding clients. Existing clients change only at renewal, 5–10%, with 30 days' notice. Never discount to close a deal.
